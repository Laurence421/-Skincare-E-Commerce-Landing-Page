Your Guide to Radiant Skin

5 Mistakes That Might Be Hurting Your Skin. Your skincare routine plays a crucial role in maintaining healthy, radiant skin. However, some common habits might be doing more harm than good. Here are five mistakes to avoid: 1. Overwashing Your Face — Washing too often or using harsh cleansers can strip......

  1. Overwashing your face. Washing too often or using harsh cleansers strips your skin's natural oils. Cleanse gently twice a day.
  2. Skipping sunscreen. UV rays cause dark spots and early ageing, even on cloudy days. Apply SPF 30+ every morning.
  3. Picking at spots. Squeezing pimples pushes bacteria deeper and leaves scars. Treat them with a targeted serum instead.
  4. Over-exfoliating. Scrubbing every day damages your skin barrier. Exfoliate 2–3 times a week at most.
  5. Skipping moisturiser on oily skin. Dehydrated skin makes even more oil. Use a light, non-comedogenic moisturiser.
